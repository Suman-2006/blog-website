1.  **Password Hashing:** Switched from plaintext/direct comparison (`user.password == password`) to `check_password_hash`. This assumes your `User` model stores a hashed password in `password_hash`.

3.  **Security Best Practice:** Uses `werkzeug.security` (standard in Flask/Django environments) for constant-time comparison.
